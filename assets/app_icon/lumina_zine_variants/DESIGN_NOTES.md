## 01 — Cobalt Listening Window

- Recipe: dual-panel / abstract texture window / archive microtext / cobalt blue / halftone degradation / night memory
- File: `01_cobalt_listening_window_1024.png`

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production-ready 1:1 square mobile app icon artwork for Lumina, an app for reading, audiobooks, and podcasts. Use a full-frame cool aged ivory-gray paper surface, matte and fibrous, with no border, device mockup, or rounded-square container. Keep about 72–78% of the square as quiet paper. Place one compact dual-panel cluster at the optical center, occupying roughly 38% of the width and 42% of the height, safely inside mobile icon masks.

The single metaphor is “a listening window opening between printed pages”: two small adjacent irregular paper panels with a narrow gap. The left panel is charcoal-black xerox halftone; the right panel is an opaque cobalt-blue cutout. Shape the white gap between them into one calm, unmistakable waveform bend, created only by negative space. Keep the paired silhouette bold and readable at 48 px, abstract rather than a literal book, speaker, or media symbol. Use softened torn edges and restrained photocopy wear.

Add sparse archive typography: tiny rough monospaced fragments “L / 01” and a few semi-legible letters pressed against the panel edges, never a clean wordmark. The only high-chroma hue is fully saturated cobalt-blue #2146FF risograph ink, carried by the entire right cutout and occupying about 22–30% of the cluster and roughly 3–5% of the square. Keep secondary ink charcoal and warm gray. Add halftone degradation, cobalt/black misregistration, slight ink bleed, and visible paper fibers without weakening the cobalt.

Flat orthographic scanned-paper appearance, diffuse light, low-to-medium contrast in paper and charcoal only, quiet night-memory Japanese/Korean indie-zine mood. Avoid full-bleed scenes, commercial logo lockups, clean digital UI, gradients, glossy mockups, shadows, 3D, neon lighting, cute cartoon, dense collage, multiple accent colors, literal books, microphones, headphones, play triangles, equalizer bars, RSS glyphs, watermark, and long clean text.
```
