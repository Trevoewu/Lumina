```text

Background: fill the entire square with solid fresh mint green (#58D6A4). Keep mint green clearly visible in all four square corners and every open area surrounding the character.

Reduce her to one soft rounded continuous silhouette. Make the single defining feature the oversized circular headphone cup and smooth headband. Her expression is calm and absorbed in listening. Use only warm peach (#F3A37E) for face and neck, and deep espresso (#29232A) for hair, headphones, and the single visible eye.
Complexity: use only 4–7 large basic shapes and at most two broad internal character color regions. Show one tiny eye appropriate to the strict side profile; omit the mouth. Merge the wispy fringe into two or three broad blunt rounded locks, never individual strands. Remove every nonessential line, outline, anatomical detail, texture, and decoration. Keep the character readable at 32 × 32.
Composition: keep the character upright, emerging from the lower-left and facing inward, filling about 80% of the square. Use a compact head-and-neck crop. Keep the headphone silhouette unmistakable.
Style: make simplification, cuteness, and lovable baby-like appeal strongest. Use large soft forms, thick rounded contours, and an ultra-clean graphic treatment. Add an extremely, extremely subtle, almost imperceptible sense of depth through a barely-there neo-skeuomorphic treatment.
Finish: show only the character on the full-canvas background, with clean surfaces and normal square outer corners.
Constraints: use exactly three semantic colors in the complete image: warm peach and deep espresso for the character, plus mint green for the background. Use no text or watermark. Add no borders, frames, cards, or presentation masks. Include one character only, with no extra subjects or scenery. Use no fragile lines, sharp hair tips, unnecessary outlines, tiny details, or decorative marks. Add no photorealistic material, dramatic bevel, glossy hotspot, deep occlusion, extrusion, strong three-dimensional rendering, or external cast shadow. Keep the background solid and uniform, with no texture, vignette, halo, or lighting variation.
```
